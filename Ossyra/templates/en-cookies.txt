Any way here is how to Make cookies 

### Ingredients

* 2 ¼ cups flour
* 1 tsp baking soda
* ½ tsp salt
* ¾ cup sugar
* ¾ cup brown sugar
* ¾ cup butter (softened)
* 2 eggs
* 2 tsp vanilla extract
* 1–2 cups chocolate chips

### Steps

1. **Preheat oven** to 375°F (190°C).
2. **Mix dry ingredients:** flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
3. **Mix wet ingredients:** in another bowl, beat butter, sugar, and brown sugar until smooth.
4. **Add eggs and vanilla** to the butter mixture and mix well.
5. **Combine** the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients slowly.
6. **Stir in chocolate chips.**
7. **Scoop dough** into small balls and place on a baking sheet.
8. **Bake 9–11 minutes** until edges are golden.
9. **Let cool** for a few minutes, then eat 🍪
